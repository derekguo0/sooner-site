# Privacy Policy

_Effective September 27, 2026_

Sooner is built so that your list stays on your phone. There's no account and no sign-up, and we don't track you.

## What happens to your photos

- When you snap or pick a photo to recognize, Sooner shrinks it (long side 1,024 px) and sends it, with a short instruction, to **Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic** to identify the item and read or estimate its expiry date.
- The photo is **not uploaded to any storage** and Sooner has no server of its own. The request is protected by Firebase App Check, which lets only the genuine Sooner app use the service.
- Sooner uses the **paid tier** of the Gemini API. Under Google's terms for paid services, Google does not use your prompts or images to improve its products, and logs requests only for a limited time to detect abuse and meet legal obligations. See the [Gemini API Additional Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).
- Sooner keeps a small thumbnail (at most 200 px) **on your device** so you can recognize items in your list. The original photo isn't saved.

## What stays on your device

Item names, categories, dates, notes and thumbnails are stored only on your iPhone (SwiftData, inside Sooner's app container, protected by iOS data protection). Deleting the app deletes them. Sooner keeps two small values in your device's Keychain — today's recognition count and whether the free first recognition was used — so these can't be reset by reinstalling.

## Anonymous usage and crash data

Sooner uses **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. These record things like "a recognition succeeded, category: food, took 2.3 s" or an error type and code. They **never include item names, photos, notes or anything you type**. Sooner doesn't ask for or use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

## Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. Sooner never sees your payment details.

## Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Children

Sooner isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions? Email **derekguo0@gmail.com**.

# 隐私政策

_生效日期：2026 年 9 月 27 日_

Sooner 的设计原则是：你的清单只留在你的手机上。无需账号、无需注册，也不追踪你。

## 你的照片会怎样处理

- 当你拍照或从相册选择照片进行识别时，Sooner 会先把照片缩小（长边 1024 像素），连同一段简短的指令，通过 **Firebase AI Logic 发送给 Google 的 Gemini 模型**，用来识别物品并读取或估算到期日。
- 照片**不会上传到任何存储**，Sooner 也没有自己的服务器。请求受 Firebase App Check 保护，只有正版 Sooner App 可以调用该服务。
- Sooner 使用 Gemini API 的**付费层**。根据 Google 对付费服务的条款，Google 不会使用你的提示和图片来改进其产品，只会在有限时间内记录请求，用于发现滥用和履行法律义务。详见 [Gemini API 附加条款](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)。
- Sooner 会在**你的设备上**保存一张很小的缩略图（最大 200 像素），方便你在清单中认出物品。原图不会保存。

## 哪些数据只留在你的设备上

物品名称、分类、日期、备注和缩略图只保存在你的 iPhone 上（SwiftData，位于 Sooner 的 App 沙盒中，受 iOS 数据保护）。删除 App 即会删除这些数据。Sooner 会在设备钥匙串中保存两个很小的值：当天的识别次数，以及首次免费识别是否已使用，因此重装 App 不会重置它们。

## 匿名使用与崩溃数据

Sooner 使用 **Firebase Analytics** 和 **Firebase Crashlytics** 了解 App 的使用情况并修复崩溃。它们记录的是诸如「一次识别成功，分类：食品，耗时 2.3 秒」或某种错误类型和代码，**从不包含物品名称、照片、备注或任何你输入的内容**。Sooner 不请求、也不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不会跨 App 或网站追踪你。

## 购买

免费试用和一次性买断都由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。Sooner 不会接触你的付款信息。

## 通知

提醒是在你的设备上安排的本地通知，不会发送到任何服务器。

## 儿童

Sooner 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不会有意收集他们的个人信息。

## 变更与联系

如果本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面及生效日期。如有疑问，请发邮件至 **derekguo0@gmail.com**。
