## What Sooner does — and doesn't

- Sooner helps you remember expiry dates. Dates read from packaging can be misread, and **dates marked "Estimated" are AI guesses based on typical storage times**. They're a reminder, not a food-safety judgment. Always check the item itself — how it looks, smells and has been stored — before eating or using it.
- **Medicine:** Sooner can read the expiry date printed on medicine packaging, but it can't tell whether a medicine is safe to take and never estimates dates for medicine. Always go by the package and your doctor's or pharmacist's advice.
- Reminders depend on iOS notification settings and your device being on. Sooner can't guarantee that every reminder is delivered.
