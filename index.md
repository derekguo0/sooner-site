# Sooner

**Snap it. Sooner remembers.** · **拍一下，就记住。**

Sooner is an iPhone app that remembers expiry dates for you. Snap a photo of anything in your fridge or cupboard, and Sooner reads — or estimates — when it expires, then reminds you before it goes bad.

Sooner 是一款 iPhone App：对着冰箱或柜子里的东西拍一张照，它会读出（或估算）到期日，并在过期前提醒你。
